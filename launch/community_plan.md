# Rake — Community Building & Engagement Plan

This document establishes the community architecture, weekly rituals, moderation guidelines, and newsletter strategy for **Rake**.

---

## 1. Community Vision: The Quietest Place on the Internet

Online spaces are frequently hyperactive, chaotic, and loud. The community for Rake is designed as an extension of the game itself: **a calm, respectful sanctuary for quiet reflection, artistic sharing, and mindful conversations**.

---

## 2. Discord Server Architecture

The official Discord server is called **"The Garden"**.

### Channel Structure

```text
├── 📜 ORIENTATION
│   ├── #welcome-and-rules     (Pristine, minimal rules emphasizing respect and calm)
│   └── #announcements         (Slow updates, release notes, new seasonal themes)
│
├── 🍵 THE TEA HOUSE
│   ├── #quiet-lounge          (Slow-mode 60s enabled; thoughtful conversation about slow living, tea, reading)
│   └── #mindful-moments       (Sharing small daily joys, photos of nature, quiet desk setups)
│
├── 🎨 THE SAND GALLERY
│   ├── #show-your-garden      (Users post screenshots & 4K exports of their raked patterns)
│   ├── #daily-pattern-club    (Discussions and creative variations on the daily prompt)
│   └── #creative-suggestions  (Community ideas for new stone shapes, sand colors, and rake tools)
│
└── 🛠️ SUPPORT & FEEDBACK
    ├── #bug-reports           (Helpful, structured technical troubleshooting)
    └── #dev-updates           (Quiet behind-the-scenes engineering logs)
```

### Community Principles (Rule Set)
1. **Slow Down**: Chat slow-mode is intentionally enabled across all channels. There is no need to rush.
2. **Kindness & Grace**: Assume good intentions. Speak with gentleness.
3. **No Promotion or Spam**: Zero self-promotion, advertising, or referral links.
4. **Respect the Silence**: If you just want to lurk, listen to ambient soundscapes, or look at garden drawings without typing, you are entirely welcome.

## 3. Weekly Community Rituals

Recurring, low-effort rituals keep players engaged without pressure:

| Day | Ritual Name | Activity & Prompt | Channel / Platform |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Wednesday** | *Ripple Wednesday* | Draw a garden inspired by water ripples, raindrops, or ocean waves. | `#daily-pattern-club` & Instagram Stories |
| **Friday** | *The 3-Minute Pause* | A scheduled 3-minute group synchronized breathing and drawing session in Discord Voice (ambient stream audio only, muted mics). | `#the-tea-house` voice room |
| **Sunday** | *Silent Sunday Gallery* | Showcase the top 5 community gardens drawn during the week. Winning gardens are featured in the in-game community gallery. | In-app Community Screen & Instagram feed |

---

## 4. "The Daily Grain" — Monthly Newsletter

A slow, beautifully designed email newsletter sent on the **1st of every month** via Beehiiv / Buttondown.

### Newsletter Structure
- **Word Count**: Strictly `< 400 words`.
- **Aesthetic**: Ivory background, serif typography, single high-resolution image of a Japanese rock garden or player drawing.
- **Sections**:
  1. *A Thought on Stillness*: A short reflection on mindfulness, wabi-sabi, or digital minimalism.
  2. *Garden of the Month*: Featured community artwork and the story behind it.
  3. *Soundscape Recommendation*: A hand-picked field recording (e.g., Kyoto bamboo grove in light rain).
  4. *What's Coming to Rake*: A quiet preview of the next seasonal theme.

## 5. User-Generated Content (UGC) Reposting Workflow

1. Monitor `#show-your-garden` on Discord and tagged posts on Instagram (`#RakeZen`, `@rakeapp`).
2. Contact the author via DM requesting permission:  
   *"We love this peaceful garden you drew. Would you mind if we shared it on our Instagram and in-app gallery with credit to your name?"*
3. Export high-resolution PNG using the developer toolchain.
4. Repost with a subtle frame and author handle tagged cleanly in the corner.
