   - Provide 1 Lifetime Master Gardener Promo Code for the creator + 5 giveaway codes for their audience.
   - High conversion rate among cozy micro-influencers (5k–30k followers) who genuinely love the aesthetic.
2. **Paid Micro-Sponsorship (€100 – €300 per creator)**:
   - Dedicated 30–60 second integration or aesthetic TikTok reel demonstrating natural sand drawing during their daily break or study session.

## 5. Paid Integration Brief Template (For Sponsored Creators)

**Key Messaging Points**:
- Showcase the physical sensation of dividing sand with the rake tine.
- Demonstrate pressing "Smooth" to wipe away the patterns like a fresh breath.
- Emphasize: *"No scores, no countdowns, no guilt."*
- Call-to-action: *"Find a quiet minute with Rake. Link in my bio to download."*

**Dos and Don'ts**:
- **DO**: Record in warm, natural morning or golden-hour lighting.
- **DO**: Turn device sound UP so the crisp gravel sound effects are clearly audible.
- **DON'T**: Use loud trending EDM or high-tempo sound effects.
- **DON'T**: Speak quickly or use urgent sales vocabulary ("Hurry up!", "Best game ever!").
