# Rake — Store Review Management & Response Playbook

This document provides tone guidelines and localized developer response templates for managing customer reviews across Apple App Store and Google Play Store.

---

## 1. Developer Response Philosophy

1. **Human & Grounded**: Always reply warmly as an independent developer (sign off with a first name). Never use corporate, defensive, or automated bot language.
2. **Never Argue**: Acknowledge the user's perspective immediately, even if the review is unfair or mistaken.
3. **Turn Bugs into Advocates**: When a bug reported in a 1-star review is patched, update your developer response to notify the user. Over 35% of users update their rating from 1 star to 4 or 5 stars when informed of a fix.
4. **Response SLA**: Aim to reply within **24 hours** for negative reviews (1–3 stars) and within **48 hours** for positive reviews (4–5 stars).

## 2. Response Templates by Scenario (English)

### Scenario A: 5-Star Enthusiastic Review
> "Thank you so much for the kind words, {{User_Name}}! It means the world to know that Rake brings a moment of peace to your day. If you ever have ideas for new sand textures or natural soundscapes you'd like to see added, please feel free to reach out anytime at support@rake.app. Wishing you many calm moments ahead! 🌿 — {{Developer_Name}}"

### Scenario B: 4-Star Review with Feature Suggestion
> "Thank you for the wonderful review and thoughtful feedback, {{User_Name}}! Adding {{feature_name}} is a fantastic idea, and I've added it directly to our design notes for an upcoming update. Thank you for helping shape the garden. Warmly, — {{Developer_Name}}"

### Scenario C: 1-Star to 3-Star Crash or Technical Glitch
> "I am so sorry for this frustrating experience, {{User_Name}}. A relaxing app should never cause stress with technical glitches. I want to investigate and fix this right away for your device. Could you please send a quick note to support@rake.app mentioning your phone model? I'm actively working on a resolution and will let you know as soon as the patch is ready. Thank you for your patience! — {{Developer_Name}}"

### Scenario D: 1-Star Monetization Complaint ("It's not 100% free")
> "Hello {{User_Name}}, thank you for sharing your thoughts. I completely understand wanting things to be free. The core sand garden, drawing tools, daily inspiration, and multiple palettes are completely free with zero banner ads or forced popups. As an independent developer, the one-time €4.99 unlock helps cover continuous updates, cloud sync, and new soundscapes without relying on invasive advertising. I hope the free garden still brings you calm moments. Thank you for trying Rake! — {{Developer_Name}}"
