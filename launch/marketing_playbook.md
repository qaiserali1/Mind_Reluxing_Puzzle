# Rake — European Marketing Playbook

This playbook establishes the marketing, organic social, community, and PR strategy for **Rake**.

---

## 1. Brand Voice & Creative Manifesto

- **Tone**: Quiet, mindful, honest, respectful, grounded.
- **Rules**:
  - **No hype phrases**: Never use *"Insane game!"*, *"Addictive!"*, *"Mind-blowing!"*, *"Download NOW!"*.
  - **No urgency triggers**: No flashing timers, no countdowns, no artificial FOMO.
  - **Aesthetic focus**: Highlighting tactile sand physics, natural gravel crunch audio, and peaceful asymmetry.
  - **Audience**: Stressed young professionals, students needing study breaks, digital minimalists, cozy gamers across Germany, France, the UK, Scandinavia, and Southern Europe.

---

## 3. Reddit Organic Strategy

Reddit requires authentic, transparent indie developer communication. Any corporate or deceptive marketing is immediately penalized by the community.

### Target Subreddits
1. `r/CozyGamers` (Primary community for peaceful, low-stress games)
2. `r/iosgaming` & `r/AndroidGaming` (Mobile gaming enthusiasts looking for premium, ad-free titles)
3. `r/somethingimade` (Maker and creative community)
4. `r/Zen` & `r/Meditation` (Contextual mindfulness tools)
5. `r/InternetIsBeautiful` (Unique digital experiences)

### Post Template 1: For `r/CozyGamers` & `r/iosgaming`
**Title**: *I got tired of games that stress me out with daily streaks and popups, so I built a calm zen sand garden app called Rake.*

**Post Body**:
> Hi everyone,  
> 
> A few months ago I realized that almost every "relaxing" mobile game I downloaded was loaded with aggressive timers, energy bars, and flashing coin popups. It felt like the opposite of relaxation.  
> 
> I wanted something like a quiet Japanese karesansui garden that lives in your pocket. So I spent my evenings developing **Rake**.  
> 
> What it is:
> - You drag your finger to leave rake grooves in textured sand.
> - Lines follow your movement with realistic gravel physics and soft haptic feedback.
> - There are zero timers, no scores, no fail states, and no popups interrupting you.
> - You can follow gentle ghost pattern guides or draw completely freely.
> - When you're done, you press smooth to sweep the sand clean.
> 
> The core sandbox is free, and there is a single one-time unlock (€4.99) for extra seasonal stones and ambient soundscapes if you want to support development.
> 
> Would love for you to try it if you need a quiet minute today: [App Store link] / [Google Play link].  
> Happy to answer any questions or take suggestions for new sand themes!

## 4. Instagram Visual Aesthetic Plan

- **Grid Theme**: Alternating 3-column rhythm:
  - Column 1: Macro close-ups of sand textures, stone colors, and bamboo tools.
  - Column 2: Minimalist typography quotes on digital rest, silence, and Japanese aesthetic terms (Wabi-sabi, Kanso, Shizen).
  - Column 3: Short looping video clip of drawing satisfying curves.
- **Story Highlights**:
  - `🌿 Daily Zen`: Daily pattern reposts.
  - `🎧 Soundscapes`: Audio previews of rain, wind, and river stones.
  - `☕ Community`: Reposts of player-created garden drawings.
  - `🛠️ Dev Notes`: Quiet behind-the-scenes drawings and updates.

## 5. European Press & Media Angles

Journalists cover games that speak to broader cultural currents. Pitch Rake through these 3 topical editorial lenses:

1. **Digital Wellbeing & Burnout Antidote**:
   - *Angle*: How micro-mindfulness apps are replacing doomscrolling among remote European workers.
2. **The "Anti-Game" Movement**:
   - *Angle*: European indie developers rebelling against freemium casino mechanics in favor of quiet digital toys.
3. **Japanese Aesthetics in Modern Software**:
   - *Angle*: Applying the principles of Karesansui (dry rock gardens) to smartphone haptics and touchscreens.
