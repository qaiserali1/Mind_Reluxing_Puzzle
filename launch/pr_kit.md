## 1. Official Press Release

**FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE**

### New Mobile Sanctuary "Rake" Reimagines the Japanese Zen Garden for Overstimulated Screens

*Indie studio launches a calm, tactile sand garden experience with zero scores, zero timers, and zero pressure—designed specifically to combat modern digital burnout.*

**BERLIN / LONDON — October 24, 2026** — Independent developer studio today announced the pan-European launch of **Rake**, a meditative sand garden application available immediately on the Apple App Store and Google Play Store.

In an era dominated by hyper-stimulating mobile games featuring aggressive push notifications, countdown timers, and casino-style reward loops, Rake offers a quiet antidote. Inspired by the centuries-old Japanese tradition of *karesansui* (dry landscape rock gardens), Rake turns the smartphone screen into a tactile bed of fine sand. Players draw their fingers across the display to carve intricate grooves with realistic sand physics, accompanied by gentle haptic feedback and soothing natural soundscapes.

There are no levels to beat, no clocks to outrun, and no penalties. Players can follow delicate ghost guidelines of traditional geometric patterns—such as concentric ocean ripples and spirals—or draw freely to unwind during brief moments between meetings, commutes, or evening wind-downs.

"Modern technology constantly demands our attention with bright red notification badges and urgent timers," said the creator of Rake. "With Rake, we wanted to build an 'anti-game'—a digital sanctuary that asks nothing of you and simply gives you a quiet space to breathe, create, and clear your mind."

Rake features 16 mineral-inspired palettes (including Kyoto Moss, Basalt Slate, and Warm Sandstone), binaural nature audio (mountain streams, bamboo flutes, and singing bowls), and a daily meditative pattern. 

### Pricing and Availability
Rake is available as a free download on iOS (iPhone, iPad, Apple Watch, and Mac) and Android. A single one-time in-app purchase of **€4.99** unlocks the lifetime "Master Gardener" collection, granting access to all premium sand palettes, soundscapes, unlimited gallery storage, and AR garden mode.

## 3. Founder Story: "The Need for Slower Software"

> "Like millions of people, I found myself ending long workdays staring at my phone, mindlessly scrolling through news feeds and social apps that left me feeling more drained than when I started. Even mobile games that branded themselves as 'relaxing' were stuffed with flashing gems, daily login streaks to maintain, and intrusive ads that shattered any sense of peace.
> 
> During a visit to Kyoto, I sat before the rock garden at Ryōan-ji. Watching the monks gently sweep lines into the gravel using wooden rakes, I noticed how the simple physical repetition brought an immediate sense of stillness to everyone watching.
> 
> I realized that touchscreens are actually the ultimate tactile surface for drawing sand patterns—if only we stripped away all the casino mechanics.
> 
> I built Rake as slower software. Software that respects your time. It doesn't nag you to come back every 24 hours. It doesn't track your location or sell your data. You open it, draw three lines in the sand, feel the subtle vibration of gravel under your finger, hear the breeze in the bamboo, and leave feeling lighter.
> 
> That's what technology should do."
