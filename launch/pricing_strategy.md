# Rake — Monetization & Launch Pricing Strategy

This document details the pricing model, monetization philosophy, European regional tiers, and A/B testing framework for **Rake**.

---

## 1. Monetization Philosophy & Competitive Analysis

Rake is positioned as a **calm, ethical, digital sanctuary**. Intrusive monetization (interstitial popups, subscription fatigue, pay-to-win mechanics) directly contradicts the core product proposition and alienates European mindfulness gamers.

|---|---|---|---|---|
| **A. Pure Paid Up-Front (€2.99 – €4.99)** | High Quality / Premium | Medium (high barrier to initial install on Google Play) | **+** Zero ad code, pristine user trust.<br>**–** Slower initial organic viral growth on social media. | Alternative Phase (A/B Test candidate) |
| **B. Freemium with One-Time Unlock (€4.99)** | Fair & Respectful | **Highest (Industry Best Practice for Cozy Games)** | **+** Zero-risk download; full zen sandbox free; €4.99 unlocks lifetime themes, unlimited saves, cloud sync, AR.<br>**–** Requires compelling premium value proposition. | **PRIMARY LAUNCH MODEL (Baseline)** |
| **C. Monthly / Annual Subscription** | Low (Heavy Fatigue) | Very Low (Severe backlash for single-player zen toys) | **+** High theoretical LTV.<br>**–** Destroys user goodwill; high churn. | Rejected |
| **D. Tip Jar / Pay What You Want** | Highly Liked | Low Revenue Predictability | **+** Warm community feeling.<br>**–** Average revenue per install < €0.25. | Secondary (Complimentary) |

## 2. The Core Offering: Free Tier vs. Master Gardener (€4.99)

### Free Experience (Generous & Fully Functional)
- Full interactive sand garden canvas with smooth multi-touch physics.
- 3 Core Rake tools (Single tine, 3-prong rake, 5-prong rake).
- 4 Classic Themes (Traditional Sand, Warm Clay, River Pebble, Dark Stone).
- 10 Built-in Inspiration Patterns with ghost overlay tracing.
- Daily Inspiration pattern rotation.
- Ambient soundscapes: Gentle Wind & Mountain Stream.
- Gallery save capacity: 5 gardens.
- Gentle, opt-in rewarded ad (max 2/day, only for previewing locked themes — zero banners or popups).

### Master Gardener Unlock (€4.99 One-Time Lifetime Purchase)
- **All 16+ Current and Future Seasonal Themes** (Kyoto Moss, Sakura Blossom, Basalt Night, Desert Dune, Arctic Snow, etc.).
- **Unlimited Gallery Storage & High-Resolution Vector SVG / 4K PNG Export**.
- **All 8 Premium Soundscapes** (Bamboo Flute, Kyoto Rain, Evening Cicadas, Temple Bells, Singing Bowl).
- **AR Sandbox Mode** (Draw on real coffee tables and floors).
- **Collaborative Gardens** (Real-time shared session with family/partner).
- **Apple Watch Companion Full Access** (Haptic breathing & daily widget sync).
- **100% Ad-Free Forever** (Completely removes rewarded ad triggers).

## 4. A/B Testing Plan

To determine optimal conversion without compromising brand integrity, run a controlled A/B test in App Store Connect Product Page Optimization (PPO) and Google Play Store Experiments.

### Test Cohorts (Minimum 1,000 installs per cohort)

- **Cohort A (Baseline)**:
  - Free download with in-app purchase at €4.99.
  - Soft paywall banner on screen after 3 days of consecutive daily pattern usage.
- **Cohort B (Value-Add Bundle @ €3.99)**:
  - Free download with introductory launch price of €3.99 (20% off badge: "Founders Launch Offer").
- **Cohort C (Pure Paid App @ €2.99 in Netherlands & Sweden)**:
  - Paid up-front download (€2.99) tested against free download in smaller test markets.

### Success Metrics & KPIs
1. **D1 / D7 Retention**: Cohort retention must not drop by more than 2% between free and paid unlocks.
2. **Free-to-Paid Conversion Rate**: Target `4.5% – 6.0%` of active D7 users converting to Master Gardener.
3. **Refund Rate**: Target `< 0.5%` on iOS and Google Play.
4. **App Rating Stability**: Maintain `>= 4.8 / 5.0` stars.
