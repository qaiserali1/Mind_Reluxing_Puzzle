# Privacy Policy for Rake: Zen Sand Garden

**Last Updated: October 24, 2026**

Welcome to **Rake** ("the Application", "we", "us", or "our"). We are deeply committed to respecting and protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy outlines how your information is handled when you use Rake on iOS, iPadOS, watchOS, macOS, and Android devices.

Rake is intentionally designed under the principles of **Privacy by Design** and the European Union General Data Protection Regulation (**GDPR**). We believe technology should bring peace of mind, not intrusive tracking.

---

## 1. Information You Provide to Us

When you interact with Rake, you may provide certain minimal information directly:
- **Garden Drawings & Patterns**: Stroke coordinates, rake widths, and palette choices you draw in the sand are saved locally on your device by default.
- **Artist Signature**: If you choose to configure a signature or nickname in Settings, it is saved locally to personalize your exported images.
- **Account Information (Optional)**: If you choose to link an account for cross-device cloud sync, we store your email address and an anonymized user identifier via Firebase Authentication. Account creation is entirely optional.

---

## 2. Information Collected Automatically (With Your Explicit Consent)

We do not collect diagnostic or analytics data without your explicit prior permission through the European User Messaging Platform (UMP) consent dialog. If you consent, the following may be collected:
- **Crash Diagnostics**: In the rare event of an unexpected crash, anonymous technical stack traces, device model, operating system version, and memory state are processed by **Firebase Crashlytics** to help us identify and fix bugs.
- **Anonymous Telemetry & Analytics**: If you opt into optional analytics in Settings, aggregate engagement events (such as session duration, pattern completion ratios, and theme preferences) are recorded by **Firebase Analytics**. These logs contain zero personal identifiers.
- **Device Information**: Technical specifications including screen resolution, system language, and operating system build are processed solely to ensure proper UI layout and font rendering.

---

## 3. Information We Strictly DO NOT Collect

To maintain your sanctuary, Rake **never** accesses or collects:
- Your real name, physical address, or phone number.
- Precise or coarse GPS location data.
- Your address book or contacts.
- Device microphone or audio recordings.
- Camera access (camera permissions will only be requested in future optional AR modes and never transmit video feeds off-device).
- Your private garden drawings (drawings remain on your hardware unless you explicitly tap "Share to Community Gallery").

---

## 4. How We Use Your Information

Any information collected is used strictly for legitimate, minimal purposes:
1. To render and persist your custom sand drawings and personal settings.
2. To synchronize your gardens across your personal devices via secure cloud storage (when enabled).
3. To diagnose technical defects and optimize application rendering performance.
4. To serve optional, gentle rewarded advertisements (strictly capped at 2 per day, only when requested to preview premium themes, with zero banner or interstitial ads).
5. To verify in-app purchases and manage entitlement to the "Master Gardener" features.

---

## 5. Third-Party Service Providers

We partner exclusively with GDPR-compliant service providers bound by strict Data Processing Agreements (DPAs):
- **Google Firebase (Google Ireland Limited)**: Authentication, Firestore database sync, Crashlytics diagnostics, and optional analytics. Data is encrypted in transit (TLS 1.3) and at rest (AES-256).
- **Google AdMob (Google Ireland Limited)**: Powers opt-in rewarded video previews. AdMob respects your consent choices and serves non-personalized ads when tracking is declined.
- **Apple App Store & Google Play Billing**: Process all in-app purchase financial transactions. We never see, receive, or store your credit card or bank details.

---

## 6. Your Rights Under the GDPR

If you reside in the European Economic Area (EEA), United Kingdom, or Switzerland, you possess extensive statutory rights regarding your personal data:
- **Right of Access**: You may request a complete copy of any data linked to your account.
- **Right to Erasure ("Right to Be Forgotten")**: You can permanently delete your synced cloud data at any time directly in the app via Settings > Account > Delete Account, or by emailing us.
- **Right to Data Portability**: You may export your patterns in open formats (PNG, SVG).
- **Right to Withdraw Consent**: You can revoke analytics or advertising consent at any moment via Settings > Legal & Privacy > Privacy Settings. Revoking consent will not degrade your core app experience.
- **Right to Lodge a Complaint**: You have the right to file a complaint with your local European Data Protection Authority (DPA).

---

## 7. Data Retention Periods

- **Local Device Storage**: Saved patterns and settings remain on your device until you clear app storage or uninstall Rake.
- **Cloud Database (Firebase)**: Synced gardens are stored until you delete the pattern or delete your account.
- **Crash Diagnostics**: Firebase Crashlytics retains crash dump logs for **90 days**, after which they are permanently purged.
- **Anonymous Analytics**: Telemetry logs are retained for **14 months** before automatic deletion.

---

## 8. Children’s Privacy

Rake does not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in certain European member states). If we discover that personal data from a child has been collected without verifiable parental consent, we will delete that information immediately.

---

## 9. Advertising & Tracking Transparency

- Rake contains **zero banner ads** and **zero intrusive popups**.
- The only advertisements present are opt-in rewarded video ads used solely to preview locked themes.
- Declining tracking via Apple App Tracking Transparency (ATT) or Google GDPR dialog will never restrict your access to the core sandbox features of Rake.
